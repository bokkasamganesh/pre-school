🌟 Regular Activities

What We Do Every Week

A rich variety of activities designed to nurture every dimension of your child's development.

🎨

Art & Craft

Children explore painting, sculpting, collage, and mixed media to express their creativity and develop fine motor skills.

📅 Every Tuesday⏱ 45 mins
🎵

Music & Rhymes

Songs, instruments, rhythms, and dance help children develop language, memory, coordination, and emotional expression.

📅 Mon & Thu⏱ 30 mins
🌳

Outdoor Play

Structured playground time with climbing, running, and team games to build physical strength, agility, and social bonds.

📅 Daily⏱ 30 mins
📖

Story Time

Interactive storytelling sessions that build vocabulary, imagination, listening skills, and a lifelong love of books and reading.

📅 Every Friday⏱ 30 mins
🧪

Science Experiments

Fun, safe experiments introduce scientific thinking. Children discover, question, and explore the world around them with wonder.

📅 Every Wednesday⏱ 45 mins
🧘

Yoga & Mindfulness

Age-appropriate yoga poses and breathing exercises help children develop focus, calm, body awareness, and emotional regulation.

📅 Every Friday⏱ 20 mins
💻

Computer Basics

Introduction to digital literacy through educational games and activities designed for young learners (Jr. KG & Sr. KG only).

📅 Every Tuesday⏱ 30 mins
🎭

Drama & Role Play

Imaginative play and drama activities build confidence, communication, empathy, and creative thinking through storytelling.

📅 Every Thursday⏱ 45 mins
