📋 How to Apply

Simple 4-Step Admission Process

1

Fill the Form

Complete the online or offline admission inquiry form with your child's details.

2

School Visit

Schedule a school tour. Meet the teachers and see our campus and facilities.

3

Submit Documents

Submit required documents and registration fee at the school office.

4

Confirmation

Receive your admission confirmation letter and welcome kit within 3 working days.

What to Bring Along

  • 🎂 Child's birth certificate (original + photocopy)
  • 🏠 Proof of residence (electricity bill/Aadhaar)
  • 💉 Immunization / vaccination record
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent/Guardian Aadhaar card copies
  • 📝 Completed admission application form
